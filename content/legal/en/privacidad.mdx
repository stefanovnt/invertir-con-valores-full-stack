Here is, in plain words, what data is processed, why, and what your rights are under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

## 1. Data controller

- **Tax ID (CIF):** B44900082

## 2. What we collect and why

| Service | Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Newsletter (Substack form) | Email | Send you the newsletter. Subscriptions and sending are handled by Substack |
| Money coffee (Cal.com) | Name, email and what you enter when booking | Manage the session and contact you about it |
| Browsing (Simple Analytics) | Aggregated, anonymous statistics | Learn which content is useful. No personal data |

We don't ask for more data than needed or use it for other purposes. We don't make automated decisions or build profiles.

## 3. Newsletter on Substack

The newsletter is sent with **Substack** (Substack Inc., United States). The signup form on this website is Substack's own and **only loads if you click "Subscribe to the newsletter"**; until then, no data is sent to Substack. When you subscribe, Substack processes your email to manage your subscription and send you the newsletter, under its own [privacy policy](https://substack.com/privacy).

## 4. Legal basis

- **Newsletter and money coffee:** your **consent**, given when you subscribe or book, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Browsing statistics:** Simple Analytics uses no cookies and collects no personal data, so no consent is needed.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Newsletter:** for as long as you stay subscribed.
- **Money coffee:** as long as needed to manage the session and any follow-up questions, then for the periods required by law.

## 7. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of **access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability**, and withdraw your consent, by writing to **Tension Studio SL, Avenida de Francia 153, Valencia (Spain)** and stating which right you want to exercise. We may ask you to prove your identity. You can also complain to the Spanish supervisory authority, the AEPD ([www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).

## 8. Unsubscribing from the newsletter

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, handled by Substack. You can also manage your subscription from your Substack account.

## 9. Minors

This website is not aimed at children under 14, and we don't knowingly process their data.

## 10. Security and changes

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the whole website. We may update this policy; the date of the last update is shown at the top.
