## 1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as the browser's local storage) are small files or pieces of data that a website stores on your device to remember information about your visit.

## 2. Cookies on this website

**This website does not use its own or third-party cookies while you browse.** That's why you won't see a cookie banner.

To learn, in aggregate, which content is useful we use **Simple Analytics** (Simple Analytics B.V., Netherlands), which **uses no cookies, collects no personal data or IP addresses and doesn't track you** across websites. That's why it doesn't need your consent.

## 3. Third-party services you activate

Some external services **don't load until you activate them** with a click. From then on they may use their own cookies:

| Service | When it loads | More information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Substack (newsletter) | When you click "Subscribe to the newsletter" | [Substack privacy policy](https://substack.com/privacy) |
| Cal.com (money coffee) | When you click the booking button | [Cal.com privacy policy](https://cal.com/privacy) |
| Social networks | When you click their links, on their own websites | Each network's policy |

## 4. How to manage or delete cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge…).

## 5. Changes

If we ever use cookies that require consent, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
