### Third-party services

Third-party services embedded in this website **don't load until you activate them**:

- **Substack (newsletter):** the signup form only loads if you click "Subscribe to the newsletter". From then on, Substack may use its own cookies. See its [privacy policy](https://substack.com/privacy).
- **Cal.com (bookings):** the calendar only loads if you click to book, and it may set its own cookies.
- **Social networks:** only when you click their links, on their own websites.

## How to change your choice

Use the **"Manage cookies"** link in the footer for analytics, or clear this site's data in your browser. Substack and Cal.com cookies are managed on their own websites or in your browser settings.
